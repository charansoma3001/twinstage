Arm layout

How the two arms stand on the table. The keep-out between them is worked out from these two numbers, so measure them on the real table.

What keeps the arms safe

  • Keep-out. In hands and primitives, no part of either arm, open jaws included, comes within half the gap of the midline. A target that would cross is moved away; one that cannot be cleared is not sent.
  • Slew limit. Every joint moves at most 60°/s, so the first move after linking is a ramp, not a jump.
  • Watchdog. An arm that hears nothing for 0.75 s holds where it is.
  • Travel. Goals are clamped 3° inside the range each arm's calibration recorded.
